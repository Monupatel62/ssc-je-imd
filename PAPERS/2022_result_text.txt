Subject: Declaration of Final Result of Scientific Assistant in Indian Meteorological
Department Examination, 2022.
The Staff Selection Commission conducted Scientific Assistant in Indian
Meteorological Department (IMD) Examination, 2022 from 14.12.2022 to 16.12.2022.
2. As per the Corrigendum No. HQ-PPI01/08/2022-PP_1 dated 07.02.2023 of the
said Examination, the Commission will declare the final results of the Examination for
the available vacancies before conduct of Document Verification. The IMD will conduct
Document Verification of the shortlisted candidates.
3. As the Computer Based Examination was conducted in multiple shifts, therefore,
as per the Para 12.6 of the Notice of the Examination, Marks scored by the candidates
have been normalized by using the formula published by the Commission vide Notice
No: 1-1/2018-P&P-I dated 07-02-2019 and such normalized scores have been used to
determine final merit and cut-off marks.

6. As per the Corrigendum Notice No. HQ-PPI01/08/2022-PP_1 dated 07.02.2023 of
the Examination, a reserve panel/ additional list of the candidates, category wise, has also
been prepared (being shared with the IMD in a sealed cover) and will be maintained and
operated by the IMD only after exhausting the names in the Select List. The “Reserve
Panel” will be valid for a period of one and half years from the date of declaration of
result.
7. Resolution of Tie cases: In cases where more than one candidate has secured the
equal aggregate marks in the Computer Based Examination, tie has been resolved by
applying the following methods one after another in the given order till the tie is
resolved:
I. Marks in Part-II
II. Marks in Part-I
III. Date of birth, with older candidates placed higher
IV. Alphabetical order in which first names of the candidates appear
8. One candidate bearing Roll Number 3206108387 has been debarred from
appearing in the Examinations conducted by the Commission. Therefore, the result of the
candidate has not been processed.
9. The final result of one candidate bearing Roll Number 8001100523 is kept
withheld due to the court order.
10. If a candidate scoring more than cut-off marks in final result of the Examination is
not shortlisted due to any reason, he/she must represent to the concerned Regional/Sub-
Regional Office of the Commission within two months of declaration of the final result.
11. Post declaration of the final result of the Examination, further process of Document
Verification and appointment formalities will be undertaken by the IMD of the
shortlisted candidates. If a candidate does not receive any correspondence from the IMD
within a period of one year from the declaration of final result, he/she must communicate
immediately thereafter with the Department. Further, the Commission will not, in any
circumstances, entertain any correspondences regarding Document
Verification/appointment formalities from the shortlisted candidates.
12. Representations received from the candidates with regard to the Tentative Answer
Keys have been carefully examined and the Answer Keys have been modified wherever
necessary. The Final Answer Keys have been used for processing of result of CBE. Final
Answer Keys will be uploaded on the website of the Commission from 07.03.2023 to
21.03.2023.
13. Marks of the shortlisted/not-shortlisted candidates will be uploaded on the website
of the Commission on 07.03.2023. This facility will be available for a period from
07.03.2023 to 21.03.2023. Candidates may check their marks by logging in through their
registered ID and password.
