### Q1. [SSC Scientific Assistant IMD 2017 - General Intelligence]
**Rain : Water :: Hail : ?**
- (A) Ice
- (B) Snow
- (C) Sleet
- (D) Frost

**Answer:** (A) Ice  
**Explanation:** This is a word analogy. Rain is made of water (liquid). Hail is made of ice (solid water). The relationship is: Rain → its composition (Water) :: Hail → its composition (Ice).

### Q2. [SSC Scientific Assistant IMD 2017 - General Intelligence]
**Complete the series: AAAAAAAB, BAAAAAAB, BAAAAABB, BBAAAABB, BBAAABBB, ?**
- (A) BBBAAABB
- (B) BBBAABBB
- (C) BBBABBBB
- (D) BBBAAABBB

**Answer:** (B) BBBAABBB  
**Explanation:** Each step, one 'A' from the left converts to 'B' and one 'A' from the right converts to 'B'. The pattern shows Bs increasing from both ends.

### Q3. [SSC Scientific Assistant IMD 2017 - Syllogism]
**Three statements are given, followed by two conclusions I and II. Assuming the statements to be true, decide which conclusion(s) logically follow(s):**

Statements:
- All cats are animals.
- All animals are living beings.
- Some living beings are plants.

Conclusions:
- I. All cats are living beings.
- II. Some plants are cats.

- (A) Only conclusion I follows
- (B) Only conclusion II follows
- (C) Both conclusions I and II follow
- (D) Neither conclusion I nor II follows

**Answer:** (A) Only conclusion I follows  
**Explanation:** From "All cats are animals" + "All animals are living beings" → All cats are living beings (Conclusion I is TRUE). Conclusion II: "Some plants are cats" cannot be derived — plants are living beings but there's no direct link to cats. Conclusion II does NOT follow.

### Q4. [SSC Scientific Assistant IMD 2017 - Number Series]
**What comes next in the series: 2, 6, 12, 20, 30, ?**
- (A) 40
- (B) 42
- (C) 44
- (D) 48

**Answer:** (B) 42  
**Explanation:** Differences: 4, 6, 8, 10, 12... (increasing by 2). Next term = 30 + 12 = 42.

- (B) EOJDJEFM
- (C) NFEDJDOF

### Q6. [SSC Scientific Assistant IMD 2017 - Blood Relations]
**A is the father of C. D is the son of C. E is the brother of D. How is A related to E?**
- (A) Grandfather
- (B) Father
- (C) Uncle
- (D) Son

**Answer:** (A) Grandfather  
**Explanation:** A → father of C → C has son D → E is brother of D → E is also C's son. So A is the grandfather of E (A's child's child = A's grandchild).

- (A) Patient
- (B) Student

- (D) Book

### Q8. [SSC Scientific Assistant IMD 2017 - Direction Sense]
**A person walks 10 km North, then turns right and walks 5 km, then turns South and walks 10 km. How far is he from the starting point and in which direction?**
- (A) 10 km East
- (B) 5 km East
- (C) 5 km West
- (D) 10 km West

**Answer:** (B) 5 km East  
**Explanation:** North 10km → East 5km → South 10km. Net displacement: 0 km North-South (10km N cancelled by 10km S), 5km East. So 5 km East from start.

### Q9. [SSC Scientific Assistant IMD 2017 - Classification/Odd One Out]
**Which of the following is the ODD one out?**
- (A) Keyboard
- (B) Mouse
- (C) Scanner
- (D) Printer

**Answer:** (D) Printer  
**Explanation:** Keyboard, Mouse, and Scanner are all INPUT devices. Printer is an OUTPUT device. Hence Printer is the odd one out.

### Q10. [SSC Scientific Assistant IMD 2017 - Letter Series]
**What comes next: A, C, F, J, O, ?**
- (A) T
- (B) U
- (C) V
- (D) W

**Answer:** (B) U  
**Explanation:** Gaps between letters: A→C (+2), C→F (+3), F→J (+4), J→O (+5), O→? (+6) = U (position 21). Pattern: increasing gaps by 1 each time.

### Q11. [SSC Scientific Assistant IMD 2017 - Number Puzzle]
**If 6 × 7 = 22, 8 × 9 = 32, 10 × 11 = 42, then 12 × 13 = ?**
- (A) 44
- (B) 52
- (C) 46
- (D) 50

**Answer:** (B) 52  
**Explanation:** Pattern: (6+7) = 13 → No. Try: 6+7+9 = 22? No. Try: 6×7 = 42, 42/2+1=22? Yes! Or simply: each pair sums → 13, 17, 21, 25... differences +4. Answer: 25+? Let's check: 13×2-4=22 ✓, 17×2-2=32 ✓, 21×2=42 ✓, 25×2+2=52 ✓. Answer: 52.

### Q13. [SSC Scientific Assistant IMD 2022 - Venn Diagram]
**In a class, 40 students study Maths, 30 study Physics, and 15 study both. How many students study only Maths?**
- (A) 15
- (B) 25
- (C) 30
- (D) 40

**Answer:** (B) 25  
**Explanation:** Students who study only Maths = Total Maths students - Both = 40 - 15 = 25.

### Q14. [SSC Scientific Assistant IMD 2022 - Mirror Image/Pattern]
**Which of the following statements about mirrors is true?**
- (A) A plane mirror creates a real image
- (B) A plane mirror reverses left-right (lateral inversion)
- (C) A concave mirror always creates a virtual image
- (D) A convex mirror creates a real image

**Answer:** (B) A plane mirror reverses left-right (lateral inversion)  
**Explanation:** A plane mirror creates a virtual, erect, laterally inverted (left-right reversed) image of the same size as the object.

### Q15. [SSC Scientific Assistant IMD 2022 - Missing Number Matrix]
**Find the missing number:**
```
| 4  | 9  | 16 |
| 25 | 36 | ?  |
```
- (A) 42
- (B) 49
- (C) 48
- (D) 64

**Answer:** (B) 49  
**Explanation:** 4=2², 9=3², 16=4², 25=5², 36=6², ?=7²=49. All are perfect squares in sequence.
