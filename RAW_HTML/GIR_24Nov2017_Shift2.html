QID : 1018 - In the question two statements are given, followed by two conclusions, I and II. You have to consider the statements to be true even if it seems to be at variance from commonly known facts. You have to decide which of the given conclusions, if any, follows from the given statements.

Statement
1 : No cars are trucks.
2 : All trucks are jeeps.

Conclusion
I : Some cars are jeeps.
II : No trucks are cars.

Options:
1) Only conclusion I follows
2) Only conclusion II follows
3) Both I and II follow
4) Neither I nor II follows
Correct Answer: Only conclusion II follows
