SSC Scientific Assistant (IMD) Exam : Held on 23 Nov 2017 Shift-1 (General Awareness)

SSC Scientific Assistant (IMD) Exam : Held on 23 Nov 2017 Shift-1

(General Awareness)

QID : 476 - Tax reforms are concerned with the reforms in government taxation and public expenditure policies which are collectively known as ______.

1) debit policy
2) credit policy
3) fiscal policy
4) profit policy

Correct Answer: fiscal policy

QID : 477 - Which among the following is not a fundamental right?

1) Right to education
2) Right to property
3) Right to freedom
4) Right against exploitation

Correct Answer: Right to property

QID : 478 - Who among the following has the power to prorogue the Lok Sabha?

1) Speaker of Lok Sabha
2) Chairman of Rajya Sabha
3) President of India
4) Chief Justice of India

Correct Answer: President of India

QID : 479 - Which among the following was not annexed by applying Doctrine of Lapse?

1) Satara
2) Sambalpur
3) Jhansi
4) Mysore

Correct Answer: Mysore

QID : 480 - Who presided over Belgaum session of Indian National Congress in 1924?

1) Sarojini Naidu
2) Mahatma Gandhi
3) Rabindranath Tagore
4) Muhammad Ali Jinnah

Correct Answer: Mahatma Gandhi

QID : 481 - Near which river was most of the Rigveda Civilizations were situated?

1) Narmada
2) Saraswati
3) Ganga
4) Godavari

Correct Answer: Saraswati

QID : 482 - The standard Meridian of India passes through which of the following city?

1) Bhopal
2) Visakhapatnam
3) Mirzapur
4) Raipur

Correct Answer: Mirzapur

QID : 483 - What is the shape of Indian Peninsular Plateau?

1) Circular
2) Quadrangular
3) Triangular
4) No option is correct

Correct Answer: Triangular

QID : 484 - The longitudinal valley lying between lesser Himalaya and the Shiwaliks are known as ______.

1) Dihang
2) Dibang
3) Duns
4) Kullu

Correct Answer: Duns

QID : 485 - The rivers deposit pebbles in a narrow belt of about 8 to 16 km in width lying parallel to the slopes of the Shiwaliks, which is known as ______.

1) Bhabhar
2) Bhangar
3) Khadar
4) Kankar

Correct Answer: Bhabhar

QID : 486 - The Ganga-Bhagirathi-Hoogly River system is the national waterway number ______.

1) 1
2) 2
3) 3
4) 4

Correct Answer: 1

QID : 487 - What is the period of cold weather season in India?

1) Mid-November to February
2) October to December
3) November to January
4) December to March

Correct Answer: Mid-November to February

QID : 488 - The ratio of milk and water in a mixture is 4 : 1. What is the percentage of milk in the mixture?

1) 80
2) 60
3) 75
4) 50

Correct Answer: 80

QID : 489 - Which of the following is not a vector quantity?

1) Displacement
2) Speed
3) Acceleration
4) Force

Correct Answer: Speed

QID : 490 - Microorganisms are classified into how many major groups?

1) 1
2) 2
3) 3
4) 4

Correct Answer: 4

QID : 491 - Which of the following metal is most ductile in nature?

1) Copper
2) Silver
3) Gold
4) Aluminum

Correct Answer: Gold

QID : 492 - In which state, the Ministry of Science and Technology has launched Pandit Deen Dayal Upadhayay Vigyan Gram Sankul Pariyojana?

1) Uttar Pradesh
2) Maharasthtra
3) Uttrakhand
4) Kerala

Correct Answer: Uttrakhand

QID : 493 - What is the name of world’s largest all-composite airplane in terms of wingspan?

1) Stratolaunch
2) Airplex
3) Airlander
4) Stratobus

Correct Answer: Stratolaunch

QID : 494 - Who is the first Indian women to tie-up with the World Wrestling Entertainment (WWE)?

1) Shadia Bseiso
2) Kavita Devi
3) Babita Kumar
4) Sakshi Mallik

Correct Answer: Kavita Devi

QID : 495 - India and Myanmar signed how many agreements during the state visit of Prime Minister of India to Myanmar in 2017?

1) 4
2) 6
3) 8
4) 11

Correct Answer: 11

QID : 496 - Which Ministry has been awarded with Swachh Bharat Inter-Ministerial Award on 2nd October, 2017?

1) Ministry of Pretroleum and Natural Gas
2) Ministry of Coal
3) Ministry of Earth Science
4) Ministry of Railways

Correct Answer: Ministry of Pretroleum and Natural Gas

QID : 497 - The Union Government has approved a 100% disinvestment of which Central Public Sector Enterprise in August, 2017 ?

1) Central Electronics Ltd.
2) Bharat Heavy Electrical Ltd.
3) Airports Authority of India Ltd.
4) Coal India Ltd.

Correct Answer: Central Electronics Ltd.

QID : 498 - Which two countries have withdrawn from UNESCO, accusing the cultural body of ‘anti-Israel bias’?

1) United States of America and Israel
2) Britain and Israel
3) United Arab Emirates and Israel
4) Iran and Israel

Correct Answer: United States of America and Israel

QID : 499 - ______ National Wildlife Action Plan for 2017-2031 was unveiled by India spelling out the future road map for wildlife conservation.

1) First
2) Second
3) Third
4) Fourth

Correct Answer: Third

QID : 500 - Gina Miller has been named which country’s most influential black person for the year 2017?

1) South Africa
2) United Kingdom
3) United State of America
4) Canada

Correct Answer: United Kingdom
